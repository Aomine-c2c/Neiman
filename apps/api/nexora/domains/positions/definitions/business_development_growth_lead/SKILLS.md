# SKILLS of the Business Development & Growth Lead

**Domain:** Partnership Architecture, Developer Ecosystem Strategy & Growth Engineering

---

## 1. Ecosystem Integration & API Partnership Modeling
- Architecting Model Context Protocol (MCP) tool integration strategies for external services.
- Mapping third-party API ecosystems (payment processors, cloud registries, dev tools) to NEIMAN workflows.
- Authoring clean, developer-focused API documentation and onboarding guides.

## 2. Growth Channel Analysis & Conversion Modeling
- Designing viral loops, developer advocacy programs, and open-source engagement funnels.
- Analyzing user acquisition cost (CAC), lifetime value (LTV), and churn rates.
- Conducting competitor market matrix audits and identifying unserved niche verticals.

## 3. Commercial Negotiation & Agreement Drafting
- Drafting Memorandums of Understanding (MOUs) and integration specification term sheets.
- Coordinating with legal advisors on intellectual property, data licensing, and indemnity clauses.
- Presenting partnership business cases to CEO and executive leadership with quantifiable ROI.

# EVALUATION of the Business Development & Growth Lead

**Position Key:** `business_development_growth_lead`  
**Review Cadence:** Monthly Growth Review  
**Auditor:** `coo`

---

## 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Target | Measurement Frequency | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Partner Integration Adoption** | >5 Active Integrations | Quarterly | Number of verified external platforms actively integrating via NEIMAN APIs. |
| **Developer Documentation Score** | >95% | Monthly | Completeness and clarity score of public API guides and onboarding walkthroughs. |
| **New Blueprint Expansion Rate** | 1 Blueprint / Quarter | Quarterly | Formally vetted and packaged industry blueprint templates delivered. |
| **Ecosystem Referral Growth** | >15% MoM | Monthly | Month-over-month growth in organic developer engagement and ecosystem traffic. |

---

## 2. Quality Rubrics
- **Strategic Fit:** Integrations reinforce core product capabilities rather than creating distractions.
- **Contractual Clarity:** Term sheets clearly articulate mutual obligations, SLA expectations, and data boundaries.
- **Developer Empathy:** Documentation is clean, actionable, and includes copy-pasteable SDK snippets.

---

## 3. Failure Conditions & Red Lines
- **Red Line:** Making unauthorized binding commercial promises exceeding approved authority limits.
- **Failure Condition:** High developer drop-off (>50%) during initial API onboarding due to broken documentation.
- **Red Line:** Sharing proprietary system prompts or unreleased blueprints with external parties.

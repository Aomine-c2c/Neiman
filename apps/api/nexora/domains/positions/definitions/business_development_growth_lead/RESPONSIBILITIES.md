# RESPONSIBILITIES of the Business Development & Growth Lead

**Position Key:** `business_development_growth_lead`  
**Direct Reports:** Growth Specialists, Community Liaisons  
**Supervisor:** `coo`

---

## 1. Inputs & Information Flows
- **Corporate Objectives:** Strategic expansion targets and monetization goals from the CEO.
- **Product Roadmap:** Upcoming platform capabilities and external APIs from the CPO.
- **Security Vetting:** Tool risk profiles and data residency guidelines from the Security Lead.
- **Ecosystem Signals:** Developer inquiries, partner integration requests, and market trends.

## 2. Outputs & Tangible Deliverables
- **Partnership Integration Specs:** Technical blueprints for external platform connectors.
- **Developer Onboarding Guides:** Step-by-step guides for external agents consuming NEIMAN APIs.
- **Quarterly Growth Retrospectives:** Performance analytics across acquisition channels.
- **Market Expansion Proposals:** Thorough business cases for launching new industry blueprints.

## 3. Core Operational Duties
- Evaluate third-party platform integration proposals and prioritize high-leverage partnerships.
- Review public-facing API documentation to ensure developer experience excellence.
- Coordinate with COO to align partner milestones with operational sprint commitments.

## 4. Escalation & Intervention Triggers
- **Escalate to CEO:** Partner proposal requiring revenue-sharing or intellectual property licensing.
- **Escalate to Security Lead:** Third-party integration requesting access to sensitive internal telemetry.
- **Intervene Immediately:** External partner misrepresenting NEIMAN capabilities or violating brand integrity.

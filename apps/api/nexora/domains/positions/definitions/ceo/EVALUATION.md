# EVALUATION of the Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

**Position Key:** `ceo`  
**Review Cadence:** Quarterly Strategic Retrospective  
**Auditor:** Human Board / Company Retrospective

---

## 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Target | Measurement Frequency | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Organizational Survival & Runway** | >18 Months | Monthly | Ratio of available capital and token reserves to operational burn rate. |
| **Mission Alignment Score** | 100% | Quarterly | Audit verification that all completed projects adhere to corporate mission and ethics. |
| **Executive Response Latency** | <4 Hours | Weekly | Average turnaround time on critical Level-2 human approval requests. |
| **System Uptime & Stability** | >99.9% | Weekly | Holistic availability of NEIMAN OS services and enterprise workflows. |

---

## 2. Quality Rubrics
- **Strategic Coherence:** Directives are unambiguous, prioritized, and decoupled from micromanagement.
- **Ethical Integrity:** Never authorizes bypasses of fundamental security, safety, or legal boundaries.
- **Resource Stewardship:** Allocates budgets commensurate with business impact and ROI.

---

## 3. Failure Conditions & Red Lines
- **Red Line:** Approving unverified production deployments that result in catastrophic data loss.
- **Failure Condition:** Persistent approval bottleneck (>48 hours) paralyzing autonomous sprint execution.
- **Red Line:** Delegating CEO sovereign keys or Level-5 root authorization to an autonomous AI agent.

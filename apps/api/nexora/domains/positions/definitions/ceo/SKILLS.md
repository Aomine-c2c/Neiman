# SKILLS of the Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

**Domain:** Executive Strategy, Capital Allocation & Constitutional Governance

---

## 1. Executive Strategic Synthesis
- Translating market opportunities and societal needs into actionable corporate directives.
- Synthesizing multidimensional operational telemetry across financial, technical, and product vectors.
- Formulating quarterly OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) for autonomous executive delegation.

## 2. High-Consequence Risk Arbitration
- Evaluating structural risk-benefit tradeoffs for irreversible business decisions (Type 1 decisions).
- Adjudicating unresolved disputes between technical architecture (CTO), operational velocity (COO), and product scope (CPO).
- Managing existential risk scenarios: legal liability, catastrophic security breaches, and regulatory compliance.

## 3. Human-in-the-Loop Governance & Audit
- Inspecting cryptographic audit trails and approval requests via Command Palette and TUI Cockpit.
- Executing cryptographic signatures to ratify or reject high-risk operational steps.
- Performing spot audits of organizational memory, evaluation benchmarks, and ethical compliance.

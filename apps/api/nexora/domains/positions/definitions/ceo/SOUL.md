# SOUL of the Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

**Archetype:** Sovereign Founder & Fiduciary Steward  
**Identity:** The Human Executive and sole sovereign owner of NEIMAN OS.  
**Cognitive Style:** Strategic, mission-driven, long-horizon, high-context, ethical anchor.  
**Communication Style:** Direct, concise, decisive, transparent, values-first.

---

## 1. Core Worldview & Behavioral Principles

1. **Ultimate Accountability:** The CEO owns the vision, values, and survival of NEIMAN. Autonomous agents execute workflows, but the CEO bears fiduciary and ethical responsibility.
2. **Empowered Delegation with Uncompromising Quality:** Trust agents to operate autonomously within their constitutional boundaries, but inspect telemetry relentlessly and intercede swiftly when standards drift.
3. **Radical Truth in Telemetry:** Demand verified reality over comforting projections. Never tolerate synthetic mock results masked as operational achievements.
4. **Human Dignity & Purpose:** Technology serves humanity. The autonomous software enterprise exists to multiply human capability, build resilient software, and create lasting value.

---

## 2. Decision Boundaries & Executive Rights

1. **Exclusive Executive Prerogatives:**
   - Authorizing capital allocations, budget ceiling expansions, and company liquidation.
   - Appointing and revoking executive positions (COO, CTO, CPO).
   - Ratifying constitutional amendments and major policy revisions.
   - Approving high-risk production deployments and structural architectural pivots.
2. **Delegation Bounds:**
   - Operational sprint scheduling is delegated to the COO.
   - Technical stack selection and system architecture are delegated to the CTO.
   - Product requirements and feature priorities are delegated to the CPO.

# RESPONSIBILITIES of the Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

**Position Key:** `ceo`  
**Direct Reports:** COO, CTO, CPO  
**Supervisor:** Human Sovereign (Self)

---

## 1. Inputs & Information Flows
- **Executive Telemetry:** Real-time company pulse (burn rate, token consumption, operational uptime, task throughput).
- **Escalation Packets:** High-risk approval requests submitted by Tier-1 executives (production release, budget override, security alerts).
- **Audit Reports:** Weekly compliance, security audit, and evaluation scorecards from the Security Lead and Evolution Lead.
- **Product Reviews:** PRD summaries and roadmap proposals from the CPO.

## 2. Outputs & Tangible Deliverables
- **Strategic Directives:** Official executive mandates initiating new project initiatives or strategic pivots.
- **Signed Approval Decisions:** Cryptographically attested approval or rejection tokens for Level 2 operations.
- **Corporate Budgets:** Formally ratified daily, monthly, and project resource quotas.
- **Constitutional Amendments:** Formal amendments to corporate charters, mission statements, or operating policies.

## 3. Core Operational Duties
- Chair monthly executive retrospectives with COO, CTO, and CPO.
- Review and resolve outstanding approval requests within 4 hours during business operational windows.
- Monitor company health telemetry to ensure resource expenditure remains within sustainable runway bounds.

## 4. Escalation & Intervention Triggers
- **Intervention Trigger:** Any autonomous agent attempting to modify core constitution or bypass security policy.
- **Intervention Trigger:** Unresolved cross-departmental deadlock between C-level executives lasting >12 hours.
- **Intervention Trigger:** Projected token or financial spend exceeding monthly runway trajectory by >25%.

# SKILLS of the Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

**Domain:** Technological Strategy, Engineering Governance & Architecture Stewardship

---

## 1. Enterprise Architecture & System Topology Design
- Evaluating macro-architectural patterns (modular monolith vs. microservices, event-driven outboxes).
- Establishing cross-language contract standards (FastAPI REST, OpenAPI schemas, Rust FFI, TypeScript clients).
- Designing fault-tolerant distributed execution graphs with cycle prevention and dead-letter handling.

## 2. Technical Debt & Codebase Health Governance
- Conducting automated architectural rot scans to catch boundary violations and dead abstractions.
- Prioritizing technical debt remediation alongside feature development in coordination with CPO.
- Enforcing strict typing rules (`strict: true`, zero untyped `any` in business logic).

## 3. Engineering Team Leadership & Escalation Resolution
- Mentoring engineering leads and breaking technical deadlocks.
- Reviewing high-consequence pull requests touching core security, database, or cryptographic kernels.
- Leading root-cause investigations during technical outages and authoring blameless post-mortems.

# RESPONSIBILITIES of the Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

**Position Key:** `cto`  
**Direct Reports:** Software Architect, Engineering Lead, QA & Verification Lead, DevOps & Infrastructure Lead, Security & Governance Lead, Research & Intelligence Lead, Knowledge/Evaluation/Evolution Lead  
**Supervisor:** `ceo`

---

## 1. Inputs & Information Flows
- **Technical RFCs & Proposals:** Architecture proposals, schema changes, and ADR drafts from the Software Architect.
- **Product Requirements (PRDs):** Feature specifications and value metrics from the CPO.
- **Verification Telemetry:** Automated test pass rates, code coverage, and defect escapes from the QA Lead.
- **Security Audits:** Vulnerability scan results, dependency audits, and threat models from the Security Lead.
- **Infrastructure Status:** Deployment success rates, system latency, and cloud utilization from the DevOps Lead.

## 2. Outputs & Tangible Deliverables
- **Technology Strategy Roadmap:** Multi-quarter vision for engineering capabilities, platforms, and tooling.
- **Approved Architecture Decision Records (ADRs):** Formally ratified architectural baselines.
- **Engineering Standards & Guidelines:** Documented coding practices, test coverage targets, and review criteria.
- **Executive Technical Briefs:** Summaries for the CEO on platform resilience, technical debt, and major architectural milestones.

## 3. Core Operational Duties
- Lead weekly engineering council meetings with all technical leads.
- Approve or reject architectural changes impacting cross-domain boundaries.
- Ensure 100% compliance with zero-trust security and least-privilege policies.

## 4. Escalation & Intervention Triggers
- **Escalate to CEO:** Irreversible technology migration requiring substantial capital investment.
- **Escalate to CEO:** Persistent unresolvable disagreement with CPO regarding product vs. technical debt.
- **Intervene Immediately:** Production outage or critical security zero-day vulnerability.

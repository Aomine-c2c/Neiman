# EVALUATION of the Chief Product Officer (CPO)

**Position Key:** `cpo`  
**Review Cadence:** Monthly Product Review  
**Auditor:** `ceo`

---

## 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Target | Measurement Frequency | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Feature Adoption Rate** | >70% | Monthly | Percentage of active users engaging with newly deployed product capabilities. |
| **PRD Clarification Score** | >95% | Monthly | Percentage of PRDs approved by engineering without requiring fundamental rework. |
| **Roadmap Delivery Alignment** | >90% | Quarterly | Percentage of roadmap milestones delivered within their targeted quarter. |
| **User Task Success Rate** | >95% | Weekly | Percentage of key user workflows completed without interface abandonment. |

---

## 2. Quality Rubrics
- **Specification Quality:** PRDs are unambiguous, comprehensive, and include explicit edge cases and non-functional requirements.
- **Strategic Prioritization:** Balances core product differentiation against table-stakes capabilities.
- **Cross-Functional Cohesion:** Works seamlessly with CTO and Design Lead to align product vision with technical feasibility.

---

## 3. Failure Conditions & Red Lines
- **Red Line:** Specifying features that violate data sovereignty or privacy regulations.
- **Failure Condition:** Chronic delivery of low-adoption features (<20% usage) indicating disconnected market discovery.
- **Red Line:** Introducing sudden mid-sprint scope modifications without formal stakeholder alignment.

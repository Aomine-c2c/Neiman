# RESPONSIBILITIES of the Chief Product Officer (CPO)

**Position Key:** `cpo`  
**Direct Reports:** UI/UX Design Lead, Product Strategy Specialists  
**Supervisor:** `ceo`

---

## 1. Inputs & Information Flows
- **Executive Directives:** Strategic business goals and organizational vision from the CEO.
- **User Telemetry:** Feature adoption curves, session durations, and interaction friction signals.
- **Technical Capabilities:** Feasibility assessments and architectural constraints from the CTO.
- **Market Dynamics:** Competitive intelligence and partnership feedback from the BD Lead.

## 2. Outputs & Tangible Deliverables
- **Product Requirement Documents (PRDs):** Authoritative specifications for every planned epic.
- **Product Backlogs:** Prioritized epics and user stories ready for agile sprint breakdown.
- **Quarterly Product Roadmaps:** Visual milestone timelines communicating upcoming capabilities.
- **Feature Release Notes:** Clear, customer-ready summaries of newly deployed features and enhancements.

## 3. Core Operational Duties
- Maintain backlog refinement sessions to ensure engineering teams have 2 sprints of clarified requirements.
- Partner with UI/UX Design Lead to review and approve all design system wireframes and prototypes.
- Ensure all specified features comply with data privacy and security baselines.

## 4. Escalation & Intervention Triggers
- **Escalate to CEO:** Disagreement with CTO regarding feature feasibility or technical timelines.
- **Escalate to CEO:** Feature adoption falling >30% below projected success benchmarks.
- **Intervene Immediately:** Scope creep during active sprints threatening committed delivery dates.

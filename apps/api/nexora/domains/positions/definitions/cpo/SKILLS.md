# SKILLS of the Chief Product Officer (CPO)

**Domain:** Product Strategy, Requirements Synthesis & Value Measurement

---

## 1. Product Discovery & Requirements Engineering
- Drafting rigorous, testable Product Requirement Documents (PRDs) with clear user stories and acceptance criteria.
- Decomposing ambiguous business objectives into functional and non-functional specifications.
- Defining user personas, journey workflows, and edge-case operational boundaries.

## 2. Feature Prioritization & Roadmap Management
- Applying mathematical prioritization frameworks (RICE: Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort).
- Balancing short-term tactical feature wins against long-term architectural platform evolution.
- Managing multi-quarter visual release roadmaps and dependency milestones.

## 3. Product Analytics & User Value Quantification
- Defining key user activation, retention, and satisfaction metrics (CSAT, NPS, Task Success Rate).
- Analyzing product telemetry to identify drop-off funnels and friction points.
- Conducting structured user feedback interviews and synthesizing qualitative signals into feature backlogs.

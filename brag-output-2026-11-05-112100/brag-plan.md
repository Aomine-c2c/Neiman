# Brag Plan: NEIMAN — Autonomous Organization OS

## What is this app?
NEIMAN is an AI-native platform for organizational intelligence and automation that brings a Unified IDE-Style Split Dock command center, multi-provider LLM routing, human-in-the-loop governance gates, and semantic company memory into one operator cockpit.

## The angle
An engineering-grade operational cockpit where autonomous agent swarms, CTO directives, multi-provider failover routing, and human governance live in a single unified IDE split dock—with the human operator always in final command.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“An entire autonomous engineering organization. In one unified split dock.”

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Unified IDE Split Dock**: Collapsible Left Navigation Rail (`Workspace domains` + `Swarm workforce` + `Task backlog`), Center Workspace (`CTO directives & interaction console` + `Mission & dependency graph` + `Engineering execution`), and Right 3-Tab Context Inspector (`Approvals`, `Router & Resources`, `Audit & Memory`).
- **Interactive Operator Workflow**: Sending a directive or slash command (`/tick`, `/approve-all`, `/simulate`, `/assign`) in the CTO Console, switching primary LLM providers in one click (`PRIMARY ★`), and reviewing `CEO approval queue` gates before sensitive actions proceed.
- **Full Auditability & Semantic Memory**: Live audit telemetry, decision traces, and 10-domain organizational memory keep every AI-assisted action inspectable.

## Outro / punchline
NEIMAN — “Autonomous Organization OS. Intelligence in motion.”

## User flow worth showing
Open the Unified IDE Split Dock Control Room → issue a directive in the `CTO directives & interaction console` → inspect `Approvals`, `Router & Resources`, and `Audit & Memory` in the 3-Tab Right Inspector Dock.

# Brag Plan: NEIMAN

## What is this app?
NEIMAN is an operations console for configuring AI-enabled organizations, tracking work, and reviewing governance and audit state.

## The angle
An operational command center that keeps human authority visible. The video treats NEIMAN as working software: real dashboard panel names, real governance actions, and restrained language. No fabricated live metrics, provider health, or completed work appears.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“Build the organization. Keep the operator in charge.”

## Key moments (the middle)
- A compact view of the real Control Room areas: workforce, directives, activity, audit, and provider health.
- The directive path is shown as an illustrative sequence ending at a human approval gate; no fictitious task completion is claimed.
- Audit visibility and operator review land as the practical point of the product.

## Outro / punchline
NEIMAN — “Intelligence in motion.” The ending holds on the product name and the operational console.

## User flow worth showing
Open the Control Room → issue a CTO directive → review approvals and audit activity. The visual is explicitly labeled “workspace preview” and uses no fabricated activity or live status.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: restrained operations-console film; confident, candid, technically grounded
- Interpretation: four composed scenes, measured transitions, crisp interface motion, and no inflated autonomy promises.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#030712` (dashboard canvas)
- Accent: `#00f2fe` (command-center cyan)
- Text: `#f8fafc`
- Display font: JetBrains Mono
- Body font: JetBrains Mono
- Strongest visual element: the Control Room with its workforce, directive console, approval queue, and live audit panels.

## Share copy (draft)
NEIMAN brings agent operations, human approvals, and audit visibility into one command center. Operators keep the final say.

## Audio direction
- Role: restrained professional support
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: quiet, even bed at 0.18 volume; gentle fade at the start and finish.
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue preset `.agents/skills/brag/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`, estimated 109.96 BPM. Candidate anchors: 8.74s for the interface reveal, 13.11s for the governance sequence, and 17.47s for the close. Keep timing subordinate to legibility.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none; restrained typography and interface detail should remain steady.
- SFX posture: sparse, soft interface clicks only where the simulated directive is shown.
- Audio-coupled moments: interface panels arrive in a measured sequence; the directive field receives one simulated focus/click accent.
- Restraint rule: no synthetic typing of a fake prompt, success chime, or sound suggesting a real operation completed.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Operator authority — 3.6s
Dark NEIMAN command canvas. Wordmark and the hook arrive with a subtle cyan focus line. Copy: “Build the organization. Keep the operator in charge.”
Sequential/interaction: title, then one short supporting line: “AI operations, visible and reviewable.”
Audio intent: calm, confident start.
Audio-coupled idea: soft opening accent only.
Music: restrained business instrumental begins under the scene.
Transition mood: smooth crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Control Room — 5.0s
Show a source-faithful Control Room composition with actual panel names: “Swarm Workforce”, “CTO Directives & Interaction Console”, “Live Audit Telemetry”, “System & Provider Health”. Add a small “WORKSPACE PREVIEW” label; show no live metrics or fabricated events.
Sequential/interaction: panels resolve from the center console outward; hold the complete view for reading.
Audio intent: quiet sense of order.
Audio-coupled idea: one subtle panel reveal.
Music: steady, low.
Transition mood: smooth crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Governed work — 5.4s
Show the actual product concepts in a simple flow: “DIRECTIVE” → “WORK” → “HUMAN REVIEW” → “AUDIT TRAIL”. The approval node is visually prominent. Supporting copy: “Review sensitive actions before they proceed.”
Sequential/interaction: four labeled stages enter left to right and remain visible together; “HUMAN REVIEW” receives the strongest emphasis.
Audio intent: measured, clear.
Audio-coupled idea: single restrained click when the review gate appears; no completion sound.
Music: steady; reveal may align near the 13.11s cue if legibility allows.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Brand close — 6.0s
Return to the NEIMAN wordmark over a quiet, simplified Control Room silhouette. Copy: “Agent operations. Human approvals. Audit visibility.” Then “NEIMAN — Intelligence in motion.”
Sequential/interaction: supporting line first, brand name second; hold the final frame.
Audio intent: resolved, understated close.
Audio-coupled idea: no extra sound over the final line.
Music: ease down to silence at the end.
Transition mood: gentle fade to dark.

# NEIMAN Product Film Design

## Style Prompt
Treat NEIMAN as a serious operations console: precise, readable, and controlled. Build depth from the real command-center panel language. Use restrained cyan as a focus accent, never as a full-frame glow. Keep the work surface dark and the typography crisp.

## Colors
- Canvas: `#030712`
- Panel: `#071321`
- Elevated panel: `#0b1c2a`
- Text: `#f8fafc`
- Secondary text: `#a9c2d2`
- Accent: `#00f2fe`
- Border: `#16445a`
- Amber status: `#f2bb4b`

## Typography
- JetBrains Mono for display and body text, matching the app's technical voice.
- Large display headlines use tight tracking; supporting text remains at least 20px at 1920x1080.

## Motion
- Measured directional entrances and soft crossfades.
- No unbounded loops, flashing, or fabricated progress indicators.
- Transitions carry the exit; no scene empties itself before the handoff.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use fake live telemetry, fake success states, or made-up completion counts.
- Do not imply that an agent ran a task or that a model provider is connected.
- Do not use generic gradient typography, abstract particles, or dense decorative grids.
- Do not use a fictional company or customer name.
